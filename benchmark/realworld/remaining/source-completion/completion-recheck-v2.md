All 36 pairs still have incomplete inventory and source obligations. Search is unresolved for 35. Resolving those issues alone would not interpret the remaining drawing.

## Implementation decision

The accumulated source-domain changes affect multiple document families, but have not closed any complete pair. Further boundary or solver changes alone do not address the independent inventory gap. The next acquisition work must preserve actual paint execution dependencies and provide interpretation or a source-backed absence proof for the affected scope. Relative paint equality and OCR confidence remain insufficient for strict text inventory.

The Schedule SE residual search component contains 143 proposals: 121 one-to-one and 22 grouped proposals, all based on inferred text similarity. Its subset search stops at the 24-proposal bound before exploring any state. Solving it could improve search, but these inferred comparisons alone cannot discharge the remaining 867/884 source references. `se-search-recheck.json` binds this observation to the raw report; do not prioritize a larger search budget as a completion fix.
