## Proof and ownership

A domain requires an already admitted padding-boundary proposal that is mandatory
without inferred suppliers. Its enclosing scope must remain conditional on source
correspondence. Existing local native closure checks inventory, glyph census,
order, paint, alternative partitions and overlapping source conflicts for each
entire parent. Both native bodies must have exact, fully source-backed projections,
with supported Unicode scalars. Every parent source must be projected before a
cut can establish an interior; unprojected evidence may not be classified as
exterior padding. A shared glyph cannot be divided by a cut.

`TextSourcePartition` then constructs the owned fragment and explicit complement.
The existing exact local comparator verifies equality. Only verified owned source
references enter text coverage; no whole-node correspondence or relationship map
is created. The existing paragraph/search and document inventory obligations
remain. A bounded proof phase uses the existing local proof-work limit, with
reserved comparison work, and does not take discovery's earlier budget.

The proof authority is private and not serialized. Reloaded JSON observations
cannot discharge source coverage without rebuilding their proof from evidence.
Normal report coverage fields describe the original in-process evaluation.

## Remaining work

The goal of at least two newly complete fixed-panel pairs remains unfulfilled.
Owning equal boundaries is the first coverage connection, not a full corresponding
multi-parent domain. Changed interiors, remaining padding, correspondence/search
obligations and complete text inventory still require separate proofs. OCR
feasibility alone does not discharge them. Unary proofs that a retained paint
operation cannot affect visible content should be investigated through execution
state, preserving the operation and its provenance rather than deleting uncertain
evidence or using relative image equality as text inventory.
