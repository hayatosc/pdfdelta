# Equal domains under source-validated padded boundaries

## Evidence that selected the change

A diagnostic build traced the equal candidates without changing their decisions.
Controller/processor supplied six candidates: each had at least one endpoint
admitted only as a padded text boundary. They were rejected before native
preparation because the whole-node equality path required ordinary accepted
correspondences. Schedule C supplied 50 candidates, including repeated locators;
both endpoint premises were ordinarily admitted, but native preparation failed.
Neither group reached the ownership-overlap check. Later failures are not implied
to be absent. Both diagnostic comparison objects matched the frozen originals.

`equal-candidate-denials.json` retains the observations. Each boundary tuple means
source-only mandatory, inferred, ordinary accepted, and padded-boundary accepted,
respectively. `equal-probe-v1-pilot.json` binds executable, instrumented source,
inputs and complete stderr logs. Production source and executable were restored
after acquisition. The diagnostic is not enabled in the implementation.

## Implemented connection

Equal candidates with padded endpoints now use the existing source-cut validator.
They declare the full matched population and the actual end/start cuts of the
two singleton boundary parents. The validator rechecks source-only admission,
the full native population, glyph projections, local closure, order and boundary
body equality. It owns only the interior and retains boundary parents, including
outer spaces, as explicit remainders. There is no new fragment-discovery rule,
paragraph-identity claim or inventory relaxation.

A standalone boundary-domain certificate is not required: a boundary's closure
alone and closure of the complete interval are different obligations. An initial
trial requiring those standalone certificates could not address any of the six
observed controller/processor candidates, because none had such certificates.
The adopted path instead revalidates the complete interval through the existing
validator. Native preparation and boundary-body failures still reject admission.

The endpoint-routing scans and source-cut construction are charged to the shared
proof budget. Changed-range review admission retains its existing path and
priority. The final exact local result must remain proved equal; no changed or
uncertain interpretation is promoted by this connection.

## Verification and result

A repeated-paragraph fixture with differing outer boundary spaces receives an
equal interior certificate. Exactly two old boundary-space sources remain
uncompared and no new-side source is omitted. Adding an unassigned glyph inside
the population rejects the certificate. Existing source-cut and native-closure
controls remain passing.

The full workspace passed 2,509 tests with two ignored. After the final endpoint
work-charge correction, the affected suite passed 89/89, generated fixtures passed
48/48, and workspace Clippy and formatting passed.

The final six-pair pilot retains identical comparison objects and coverage to
`equal-domain-v1`. It adds no equality certificate, strict source reference or
complete pair. Thus the newly connected proof path has not resolved the natural
candidates' remaining native obligations. Schedule C's projection/closure failure
and controller/processor's remaining full-population and body checks still need
investigation; absence of a certificate does not identify their exact cause.
