# Residual work: publication-ready follow-up drafts

These drafts keep unresolved cases attached to their measured denominators.
They do not waive a required acceptance gate or claim that the current engine
compares every PDF completely. No external issue has been published from these
drafts.

## Source evidence for unkeyed semantic changes

**Problem.** CSF sentence changes, DSA's added note, and form-heavy passages can
remain unresolved even after local order or a likely correspondence is known.
The literal objective may have multiple optimal source masks, and an unkeyed
unmatched paragraph does not establish deletion after movement or renaming.

**Evidence.** [The fixed source contract](audited-source-contract.json),
[source fixtures](../../../../fixtures/issue12/README.md), and
[the current structure probe](final-structure-claims.json) preserve these cases.
The probe distinguishes caller-supplied domains from discovered correspondence.
CSF's frozen mask costs 178 edits against a literal optimum of 158; the DSA
introduction retains 33 ambiguous source positions after 74 mandatory ones.
These are contract limitations, not permission to rewrite the annotations.

**Proposed work.** Identify independently checkable scope membership, explicit
roles, and source identity that could discriminate competing paragraph histories.
Retain key absence, element identity, exact character changes, and inferred
interpretations separately. Do not make similarity or one selected edit script
an established correspondence premise.

**Acceptance.** Preserve the existing frozen annotations and all failure rows;
add source-backed changed and unchanged controls that distinguish the proposed
premise from rename, movement, split/merge, duplicate-key, and missing-inventory
alternatives. Require a complete source mask for a whole-event claim. Freeze and
test on a new series after any holdout-informed implementation change.

## Bounded visibility observation with source provenance

**Problem.** Unsupported clipping and incomplete acquisition leave much of some
documents undiscovered. Rendering an image does not establish complete native
text extraction or a glyph-to-operator correspondence.

**Evidence.** PostgreSQL remains a 69-glyph native inventory on each side in the
baseline reproduction; only 39 glyphs are compared. The corpus reports retain
clipping issues and incomplete inventories. Extraction boundaries preserve
neighboring glyph IDs without inventing missing text or bounding boxes.

**Proposed work.** Begin with one bounded observer experiment against the pinned
`hayro-interpret` 0.7.0 device callbacks. Establish an object/operator/Form
invocation bridge for visibility evidence before attempting any recovery claim.
Coordinate proximity alone is insufficient. Preserve parser and worker limits;
do not add a PDF object parser or silently ignore unsupported paint operations.

**Acceptance.** Source-backed clipped, nested-Form, soft-mask, and independent
unaffected-region fixtures must distinguish observed visibility from uncertain
text inventory. Every observer limit must retain an actionable unresolved
dependency. Re-evaluate the affected full PDFs without removing failed channels
or weakening the exact-source contract.

## Visual candidate work under the existing pixel budget

**Problem.** The same-grid visual supplier can exhaust its 32,000,000-sample
budget before all candidate pairs are examined. The current change does not
implement an image-fingerprint optimizer or certify unexamined rivals.

**Proposed work.** Measure the eight-page full-grid workload before adding a
bounded source/context index. Cache cheap features to order or exclude candidates
only when the exclusion has a valid premise. Approximate fingerprints can rank
comparisons; exact claims still require the retained samples and transforms.

**Acceptance.** Include the shifted-foreground/blank counterexample and small
numerical/sign changes in Rust fixtures. Preserve ambiguity when rivals remain
unexamined. Report work and memory independently of recall; downsampling or
arbitrary warping cannot certify unchanged content.

## Literal whitespace coordinates in annotation files

**Problem.** Version 1 annotation quotes collapse whitespace. A genuinely changed
trailing source space therefore cannot be selected by its literal offset.

**Evidence.** The retained [Form 3800 annotation](unseen7-irs3800-expected.json)
has three independently localized changed source scalars, including old
`[95, 96)`. [Its source preflight](unseen7-ninth-replacement-literal-preflight.json)
passes, but annotation validation rejects that range after collapsing the quote.
The failed trial remains in [the selection record](unseen7-selection.json).
It was rejected before any comparison output, rather than trimmed into a pass.

**Proposed work.** Add an explicitly versioned literal-coordinate quote mode.
Preserve version 1 behavior and existing frozen annotations. Bind literal
scalars to raw source atoms, and distinguish actual whitespace from synthetic
layout separators and expanded glyphs. Keep this protocol in the benchmark;
production comparison must not read annotation boundaries or expected IDs.

**Acceptance.** Cover leading/trailing spaces, repeated spaces, tabs, newlines,
ligature expansion, source-free separators, ambiguous repeated characters, and
input-hash mismatch. Require selected source sets and changed-count bounds to
agree before whole-event scoring. Re-evaluate the original failed annotation
without changing its intended source mask; report it as development evidence.
